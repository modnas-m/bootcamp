Stadium Façade

BIM To Fabrication

The client asked for advisory in the creation of a precise Revit model for the façade structure of a stadium. Along the process this evolved into two tasks: I) the generation of a full, precise BIM model that allowed for conflict detection (within the system as well as with other disciplines, like the main stadium structure) and 2) the creation of fabrication models reading data off the base model.

All processes were designed and automated through Rhino.Inside Revit and Grasshopper, allowing the team to leave out fabrication specific software like Solidworks.

Island Topography

Urbanism and topography BIM package in Saudi Arabia

The client needed a team with both BIM and complex modelling expertise to be able to carry out the earthwork and urbanism tasks on a Revit model.

Working alongside the urban planners and architects on a live project, the topography as well as roads, paving, urban furniture, urban energy systems etc. were introduced through Rhino. Inside and multiple Grasshopper scripts.

Heterotopy

Critical scattered penitentiary complex in Alfama, Lisbon

Heterotopy is proposed as a penitentiary system based on the social division of its subjects and their punishment by means of sordid architectures. In contrast to this, the proposal manifests a hypothetical reality in which a penitentiary complex occupies the old town of Lisbon, superimposing a penitentiary plot to the civil grid and weaving both realities together.

Syntax

Crisis residential module in Barcelona

SYNTAX seeks to be a system that generates space according to need, rather than limiting itself to a specific form. This allows the project to retain a degree of uncertainty both spatially and temporally and, therefore, to be located within the conceptual framework of emergency housing—change, versatility.

The proposal takes the form of a lightweight platform frame system in which the number of variations of structural elements has been minimized in terms of cost and constructive effectiveness.
